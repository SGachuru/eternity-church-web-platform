About us

We are a church
built on Christ.

Founded on the Rock, Jesus Christ, Eternity Gospel Church and Ministries is a Pentecostal and Evangelistic community focused on biblical truth, prayer, worship, and discipleship.

01Our story

Rooted in the Word,
led by grace.

Eternity Gospel Church and Ministries is an autonomous Pentecostal and Evangelistic Ministry committed to the proclamation of the Gospel and the spiritual growth of every believer. We believe Jesus Christ is the foundation of our faith, the center of our worship, and the anchor for all ministry.

Our mission is to teach the Word of God, pray with power, serve the community, and touch the world with the Love of Christ. We welcome people from every background and invite them to encounter the transforming power of God.
